HOW IT WORKS

From claim to assessment

SatyaCheck combines language understanding with risk-pattern analysis.

01

Enter the claim

Provide a headline and the news, article or message you want to analyze.

02

Language analysis

The text is processed and passed through the fine-tuned MuRIL transformer.

03

Risk detection

Additional signals such as urgency, suspicious links and claim patterns are checked.

04

Final assessment

The system returns a classification, confidence score and detected signals.

ABOUT SATYACHECK

Built to encourage responsible sharing.

SatyaCheck is an AI-powered news analysis project focused on Hindi, English and Hinglish content.

It uses a fine-tuned MuRIL transformer together with additional risk-pattern analysis to identify potentially misleading content.

SatyaCheck is an educational and research project. AI predictions should not replace independent verification of important claims.
